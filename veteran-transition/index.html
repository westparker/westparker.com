Veteran transition

Key resources and programs worth considering, based on your goals. Dates shift a little each year, so confirm on each site before you plan around them.

MBA scholarships

  • LunaCap Foundation Opens Jan 1, due Mar 31

    MBA scholarships for active-duty military and veterans. You must be admitted to an MBA program to apply.

  • Pat Tillman Foundation Opens Dec 1, due Feb 1

    Tillman Scholars get academic funding, leadership development and a lifelong network. You need a FAFSA on file.

  • Glen Doherty Memorial Foundation Due in July

    For current and former special operations professionals. Covers degrees and professional training.

  • Your MBA program

    Always ask for more funding. Many schools have dedicated veteran scholarships, and the first offer is not always the final one.

There are plenty of others. These are the ones to start with.

Getting into an MBA

  • Service to School (S2S)

    Free MBA application mentorship from veterans who have been through it.

  • Enlisted ExFil

    Support for enlisted service members applying to college and MBA programs.

  • GRE prep

    Smart with a Heart, Sherpa Prep, Target Test Prep (TTP) and GregMat are all strong options.

Transition programs

  • The Honor Foundation

    A three-month, in-person course that meets twice a week. Built for Special Operations Forces.

  • Special Operators Transition Foundation (SOTF)

    An online, self-paced transition course for Special Operations and select intelligence personnel.

  • Deloitte CORE Leadership Program

    A two-and-a-half-day leadership and networking program at Deloitte University in Texas, with travel covered.

  • BreakLine

    Career transition focused on job placement in tech and defense tech.

  • FourBlock

    A free, ten-week career readiness program, in person or virtual.

Mentorship and networks

  • American Corporate Partners (ACP)

    Pairs you with an industry mentor for a year. Choose carefully: you only get one.

  • 51 Vets

    Mentorship, city summits and career placement for Special Operations and aviation veterans heading into finance, consulting and venture.

SkillBridge and internships

Start networking early with the company you want. A few to look at:

  • McKinsey, Bain, BCG and Deloitte

    All run SkillBridge programs. Other consulting firms may have openings too.

  • Blackstone MINT

    A pre-MBA military internship in financial services, based in New York.

  • Guggenheim VTAP

    A paid, ten-week summer internship for transitioning veterans, based in New York.
