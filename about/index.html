About

I'm a former Green Beret. I spent a decade leading teams and solving hard problems across the United States, Europe and Asia, much of it in the Indo-Pacific, and I speak Mandarin Chinese.

I come from a military family and went to Wofford College on an ROTC scholarship. Today I'm finishing a full-time MBA at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business, concentrating in Applied AI, Finance, Strategic Management and General Management, and working as a consultant at Deloitte.

On the side I build software. Some of it is coursework, some of it is me learning Rails and JavaScript the slow way, and some of it is built quickly with AI to see if an idea holds up. You can try the live ones on the Apps page.

I also spend time helping other veterans make the move into business and tech. If that's you, get in touch.
